1. Commitment to Confidentiality

At smartzone interior, we prioritize the confidentiality and safety of our Clients' personal information. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, and protect your data when you visit our website, complete consultation requests, or subscribe to our newsletter channels. We are committed to maintaining the highest ethical and design standards, both in physical environments and digital interactions.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that is voluntarily provided by our Clients. This occurs through active input fields on our web platforms, including:

  • Consultation Requests: First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and descriptive project messages.
  • Newsletter Subscriptions: Direct Email Addresses.
  • Analytical Logs: Standard metadata such as browser type, referring pages, and session duration to improve web responsiveness.
Confidential design consult spaces representing privacy and premium architectural boundaries. Fig 2.1

3. How We Process Your Data

We believe in data minimalization. Since this platform runs as a static landing page, we do not store your credentials in a persistent online database. Instead, your form submissions are processed dynamically on the client-side and immediately redirected to our secure WhatsApp channel or standard direct email routing. This prevents leakages and unauthorized access to your private information.

4. Security and Safeguards

We implement industry-standard security practices to protect your physical drawings, design files, and contact information. Our systems utilize SSL encryption for all web traffic, and physical blueprints are held securely inside our drafting archives, protected by physical access controls and designer security cabinets.

Secure custom interior installations protecting valuable Client documents. Fig 2.2

5. Compliance with UAE Data Laws

In accordance with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on Personal Data Protection, we ensure that Clients retain full rights over their personal data. You have the right to request a summary of the data we hold, request corrections to your contact info, or request complete deletion of your records from our mailing lists at any time by contacting our data officer.

6. Third-Party Disclosure & Consent

We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise disclose Client contact details to third-party advertising companies or contractors. Your details are solely used to coordinate fit-out execution, arrange scheduling, and provide relevant updates regarding your smartzone project. By utilizing our forms, you consent to this processing structure.

For inquiries, opt-outs, or updates, please contact us directly at: info@smartzoneuae.com.
