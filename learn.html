    Meet Our

    Shakir

    Founder & Chief Executive Officer

    Shakir's journey into the world of interior design began not as a career choice, but as an innate calling — a deep-seated passion for transforming raw spaces into living, breathing narratives. Born and raised with an appreciation for both traditional Arabic architecture and the sweeping modernism that defines the UAE's skyline, Shakir founded Smartzone Interior Design in 2010 with a singular mission: to create spaces that tell stories.

    With over 15 years of hands-on experience spanning residential villas, luxury apartments, corporate headquarters, retail showrooms, and hospitality venues across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, and Sharjah, Shakir has cultivated an unmatched depth of knowledge in architectural planning, spatial design, and project execution. His academic grounding in architecture provided the technical foundation, while years of collaboration with master craftsmen across the region forged an intuitive understanding of materials, light, proportion, and cultural context.

    Under his leadership, Smartzone Interior Design has completed over 200 landmark projects, earned international recognition, and built a reputation synonymous with trust, innovation, and breathtaking results. Shakir's design philosophy rests on a guiding belief: that every space should be a reflection of its inhabitant's aspirations — a perfect fusion of Eastern heritage and Western contemporary aesthetics. He remains personally involved in every major project, ensuring that each client receives not just a design, but an experience.

    "Design is not merely about beauty — it is about creating an environment where people feel the fullness of who they are. The greatest spaces do not impress; they inspire."

    — Shakir, Founder & CEO

    Our Journey

    Company Milestones

    From a bold vision in 2010 to a celebrated UAE design house — every milestone is a testament to our unwavering commitment to excellence.

    2010
    Founded
    Smartzone Interior Design is established in Dubai by Shakir, with a vision to redefine interior design in the UAE.
    2012
    First Major Dubai Project
    Completed the landmark Al Wasl luxury villa project in Dubai, setting a new benchmark for high-end residential interior design.
    2015
    Abu Dhabi Expansion
    Expanded operations to Abu Dhabi, bringing our signature blend of heritage and modernity to the UAE's capital city.
    2018
    100th Project Milestone
    Celebrated the completion of our 100th project — a diverse portfolio spanning luxury villas, corporate offices, and hospitality venues.
    2020
    International Recognition
    Received international design excellence recognition, cementing Smartzone's reputation as a world-class interior design firm.
    2023
    200+ Projects Delivered
    Surpassed the 200-project mark with an ever-growing roster of satisfied clients across residential, commercial, and hospitality sectors.
    2025
    Present Day
    Continuing to lead the UAE's interior design landscape with an expanded team, cutting-edge design processes, and an unwavering passion for craft.
    What Drives Us

    Our Core Values

    Every design decision, every client interaction, and every project milestone is guided by four foundational pillars that define who we are.

    Excellence

    We pursue perfection in every detail — from concept to final installation. Mediocrity is never an option; we deliver only the finest standard of craft and execution on every single project.

    Integrity

    Honesty and transparency form the bedrock of our client relationships. We provide clear timelines, realistic budgets, and unwavering commitment to delivering exactly what we promise.

    Innovation

    We embrace emerging design technologies and fresh aesthetic philosophies, blending timeless principles with modern creativity to craft spaces that feel both timely and enduring.

    Client-First

    Your vision is our north star. We listen deeply, collaborate closely, and tailor every element to reflect your unique lifestyle, personality, and aspirations — creating spaces that feel authentically yours.
